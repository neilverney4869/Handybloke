Hi, I'm Neil Verney

Your Canberra bloke for getting things done

Most people know me as "the helpful bloke." I'm a Canberra-based handyman and technology specialist with a lifelong love of finding a solution. If something needs fixing, setting up, or figuring out, I genuinely enjoy getting it done properly.

Born in Bulawayo, Zimbabwe, I've lived and worked in Namibia, South Africa, Europe, the UK, and Australia — immigrating here in 1996. Those experiences shaped a practical, adaptable, and down-to-earth approach to everything I do.

"My goal is simple: to help families and the wider Canberra community with honest, reliable, and reasonably priced property maintenance and technology services."
⛵ Qualified river guide

After building a website for my partner's business and project-managing our own home's knock-down rebuild, I realised I'd built up a wide range of practical skills. That's when I started The Helpful Bloke.
